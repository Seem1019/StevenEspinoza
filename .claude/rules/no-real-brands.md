# No real brands, no invented facts about Steven

- Demo brands, logos, products, restaurants, clients and testimonials are fictional. Never use names, logos, slogans or signature colour schemes of real companies (e.g. Temu, Shein, AliExpress, Amazon, Apple, Sony, Bose, Stripe, Calendly…), even as "inspiration" in visible copy.
- No photos of real people. Avatars = initials or abstract illustrations. Illustrations are drawn in the project (SVG/CSS); any third-party asset must be royalty-free and credited in `docs/credits.md`.
- Sample data that could pass as real (ratings, metrics, client logos, testimonials, "X people viewing") is labelled as sample/demo data or covered by the DemoBar notice.
- About Steven: only data in `src/data/profile.ts`. No invented clients, years of experience, awards or metrics. Missing data stays `TODO_*`.
- The agents showcase is a scripted simulation. No copy may claim content is being generated by AI in real time.
